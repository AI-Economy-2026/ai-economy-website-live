Executive Summary

This SEO audit reveals significant untapped growth potential for Wood Property (woodproperty.com.au) in the competitive Melbourne apartment market. Currently generating just 1,050 organic visits per month, the site is virtually invisible compared to local competitors — and is missing 561,891+ keyword opportunities that rivals are actively capturing every day.

Of the 13 tracked keywords, only 1 ranks in the top 3 — "sell an apartment" at #2. Three more keywords sit beyond page 1, and 9 keywords with a combined 79,000+ monthly searches are entirely absent from Google results. This includes high-intent terms like "apartment" (60,500/mo) and "rent a apartments" (6,600/mo) — searches that your competitors are capturing instead.

Compounding the issue: Wood Property's mobile performance score is 46/100, with a Largest Contentful Paint of 17.9 seconds — nearly 7x over Google's recommended threshold of 2.5 seconds. Google uses Core Web Vitals as a direct ranking signal, and this score is actively suppressing your positions across every keyword category.

The good news: the site's SEO score is a perfect 100/100, meaning the technical foundations are solid. The problems are fixable — and with targeted SEO investment, Wood Property has a clear path from 1,050 to 5,000+ monthly organic visitors within 6 months, capturing high-intent buyers and renters before they find a competitor.
