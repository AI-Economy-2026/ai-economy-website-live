Executive Summary

This comprehensive SEO audit of nmgs.com.au reveals a business with genuine competitive strengths — 50+ years of experience, 13 named enterprise clients (Linfox, Mondelez, Penguin Random House), CM3 accreditation, and a clean WordPress/Rank Math technical foundation — that is significantly underperforming in organic search due to a cluster of fixable structural issues.

The site's overall SEO Health Score is 42 / 100. The most damaging issues are concentrated in four areas: no LocalBusiness schema (meaning Google cannot resolve NMGS as a local business entity), a dual address conflict on the contact page actively suppressing local pack eligibility, PHP 7.4 running in production (end-of-life since November 2022, unpatched, publicly exposed in response headers), and Core Web Vitals estimated to be failing on mobile with a 625ms server response time and 17 render-blocking JavaScript files.

On the content side, all 22 blog posts carry zero author attribution — a direct E-E-A-T failure under Google's September 2025 Quality Rater Guidelines — and the About page at 493 words falls below the minimum threshold for the most E-E-A-T-sensitive page on any B2B services site. The site is also completely invisible to AI search systems: no llms.txt file exists, no Service schema, no FAQ content on most pages, and the GEO score is 28/100.

The good news: none of these issues require a site rebuild. The 7 critical fixes listed in Phase 1 of the action plan can be implemented within 5–10 working days with low-to-medium effort. Completing Phase 1 alone is projected to lift the SEO Health Score to approximately 58–62 / 100 and materially improve local pack visibility, Core Web Vitals, and AI search citation frequency.

Performance Root Causes & Fixes
1. Enable Elementor Improved Asset Loading
Elementor → Settings → Performance → Improved. Loads widget JS/CSS only on pages that use those widgets. Estimated LCP improvement: 1–2s.
2. Upgrade PHP 7.4 → 8.2+
PHP 8.2 is 15–25% faster for WordPress/OPcache workloads. Directly reduces TTFB from ~625ms toward the <200ms target.
3. Enable W3TC Page Cache (Disk: Enhanced)
W3 Total Cache is installed but not serving cached HTML. Enable Page Cache module. Target: TTFB under 100ms for cached responses.
4. Convert Images to WebP + Compress
Zero images use WebP/AVIF. Three CSS slider backgrounds are 1.5–1.9MB each. Convert with ShortPixel or Imagify. Estimated saving: 70–80% image payload.
5. Change Google Fonts to display=swap
Eliminates Flash of Invisible Text (FOIT) and reduces CLS. Change in Elementor → Settings → Style → Google Fonts. 5-minute fix.
6. Consider Cloudflare (Free Tier)
Edge CDN, Brotli compression, and HTTP/3 — dramatically reduces TTFB for Australian users without infrastructure changes.

Why Performance Directly Costs Rankings & Leads

A TTFB of 625ms means the server takes over half a second before sending the first byte — and then 17 render-blocking scripts must download and execute before paint begins. Google's research shows that as page load time increases from 1s to 5s, the probability of mobile bounce increases 90%. For a B2B services business where the average lead is worth thousands of dollars in project value, each second of delay is a direct revenue cost. The three highest-impact fixes — PHP upgrade, Elementor conditional loading, and W3TC page cache — require no redesign and can be completed in under a day.

AI systems cite passages that answer a user query in the first 50 words, include the entity name, geography, and a differentiator.
