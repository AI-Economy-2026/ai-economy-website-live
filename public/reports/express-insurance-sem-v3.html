Key Findings — Accountant Targeting Optimisation Opportunities
Data: Feb – May 2026 (asset group) · Mar – Apr 2026 (landing page) · 1 Apr – 11 May 2026 (geography)
1
Cross-network traffic is driving volume, but needs tighter qualification
Optimisation Area
▾

The Accounting asset group has generated meaningful reach and traffic, but the conversion quality is not yet as strong as Search. GA4 shows Cross-network traffic to the accountant landing page delivering high session volume, while Paid Search delivers stronger conversion engagement.

Cross-network in GA4 typically includes Performance Max and other Google cross-network campaign activity. To keep the debrief precise, this should be described as Cross-network / likely PMax traffic unless confirmed directly by Session campaign or Google Ads campaign name in GA4.

Review audience signals Confirm campaign source in GA4 Optimise reach quality
2
Landing page performs strongly for high-intent Paid Search traffic
Positive Finding
▾

The accountants landing page (/professional-indemnity-insurance/accountants-insurance) shows strong engagement from Paid Search visitors, with a 23.7% session key event rate and a lower bounce rate than Cross-network traffic.

This indicates the page is capable of converting when users arrive with high intent. The opportunity is to bring more qualified traffic into the page and use the Search learnings to guide cross-network optimisation.

Paid Search: 23.7% key event rate Paid Search: 32.2% bounce rate Use Search as benchmark
3
Brisbane and selected NSW areas are key locations to monitor
Geo Insight
▾

From 1 April to 11 May 2026, Brisbane City was the top converting location with 6 conversions. Sydney, Inner West Council and Rouse Hill also contributed conversions, with Inner West Council and Rouse Hill showing growth from smaller bases.

This gives a clear direction for monitoring future performance by location, while avoiding over-adjustment before campaign-level and location-level conversion data are reviewed together.
