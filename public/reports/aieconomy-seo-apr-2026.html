Executive Summary

aieconomy.ai earns an overall SEO Health Score of 50 / 100 — a site with a compelling proposition and a credentialed founder, held back by a cluster of fixable technical and content gaps. The platform's core positioning around AI strategy, training, and tools is sound, but the execution leaves significant organic potential unrealised.

The most revealing finding: aieconomy.ai sells a GEO Audit product — helping clients get cited by AI search engines — yet scores 52 / 100 on its own GEO readiness. There is no llms.txt file, no Organization schema on the homepage, no Person schema for founder Sarah Balmer, and the homepage has zero structured data of any kind. Fixing the site's own signals is the single most powerful demonstration of the product's value.

On the technical side, the site is well-hosted (Vercel edge, 74ms TTFB), correctly server-side rendered, and uses a clean URL structure. However, a 307 Temporary redirect leaks link equity; canonical tags, the sitemap, and the live serving domain all disagree on whether www or non-www is canonical; and the total image payload is 22.86 MB — nearly 10× what it should be — with no WebP conversion, no lazy loading, and no layout-shift prevention. Core Web Vitals are at serious risk as a result.

Content depth is the other major gap. The /about and /resources pages both 404 despite being linked in the primary navigation. The AI Consultancy page — the site's most commercially important URL — contains just ~308 words. There is no blog, no case studies, no long-form editorial content. The site cannot build topical authority or earn AI citations without it. The good news: the sprint-based action plan below can move the score from 50 to 80+ within 90 days.

Critical Issues

# Issue Category Effort Finding & Fix
C1 Canonical Domain War (www vs. non-www) Technical MEDIUM Canonicals → non-www. Sitemap → non-www. robots.txt Host → non-www. But live site serves www with a 307 Temporary redirect. Google must guess. Fix: Standardise on www. Change 307 → 308 in vercel.json. Update all canonicals and sitemap.
C2 Zero Structured Data on Homepage Schema LOW Homepage has 0 JSON-LD blocks. No Organization, WebSite, or Person schema. Blocks Knowledge Panel eligibility. Fix: Add Organization + WebSite + Person JSON-LD to root layout.tsx.
C3 No Favicon On-Page LOW Zero <link rel="icon"> tags. Browser shows blank tab. Google omits brand favicon from search results. Fix: Add favicon.ico to /public. Add icon metadata to app/layout.tsx icons field. ~10 minutes.
C4 H1 Hidden at -531px on Mobile Mobile MEDIUM Playwright confirmed H1 ("AI That Works") has computed top: -531px on mobile. Google's mobile-first crawler sees no above-fold headline text. Nav links have 0×0 dimensions — mobile navigation is completely broken. Fix: Restructure mobile layout: H1 → subhead → CTA → image. Implement hamburger menu.
C5 /about and /resources are 404 — linked in nav Content HIGH Both are in the primary navigation and return 404. /about is explicitly assessed by Quality Raters for E-E-A-T. /resources creates a crawl dead-end. Fix: Build both pages or remove the nav links immediately.
C6 $XX Placeholder Pricing Live on Production Trust LOW Priority AI product page shows "$XX" for two modules on the live site. Quality Raters flag unfinished pages. Fix: Replace with real pricing or "Contact for pricing."
C7 No llms.txt (site sells GEO services) GEO LOW 404 at /llms.txt. Prospects evaluating the GEO Audit product will check. The absence is the clearest possible credibility contradiction. Fix: Create llms.txt with entity statement, services, founder declaration, and RSL 1.0 license.
C8 Missing Canonical on /product/ai-toolkit Technical LOW Sitemap priority 0.9 product page has no <link rel="canonical"> and no og:url. Every other product page has both. Fix: Add via Next.js generateMetadata() on the ai-toolkit route.
